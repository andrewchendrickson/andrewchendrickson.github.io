About

I serve as Chief of the Federal Communications Commission's Office of Engineering and Technology (OET), where I advise on spectrum policy, national security matters, emerging technologies, equipment authorization, experimental licensing, and the technical frameworks shaping America's communications infrastructure.

Previously, I served as Chief Technology Officer for the FCC's Enforcement Bureau, leading technical initiatives involving cybersecurity, national security, communications networks, and critical infrastructure. Before joining the FCC, I spent more than two decades in the technology and telecommunications industries, including leadership roles at Verizon supporting 5G deployment and cloud infrastructure. My experience spans wireless communications, Geographic Information Systems (GIS), geospatial technologies, artificial intelligence, cloud computing, software-defined networking, and cybersecurity.

I remain active in open-source communities, technical research, industry collaboration, academic advising, lecturing, and public speaking, with a particular interest in the intersection of emerging technologies, communications policy, and national security. Outside of work, I enjoy audio engineering and playing music.
